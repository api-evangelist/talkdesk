Credit unions can enhance member satisfaction by modernizing customer experience through digital innovation and AI. The article identifies five improvement strategies: AI analytics for member insights, self-service capabilities, real-time AI assistance for agents, omnichannel communication, and ongoing quality management, noting that strong CX drives member retention and operational efficiency.
