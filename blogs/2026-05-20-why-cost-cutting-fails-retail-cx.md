Aggressive cost-cutting in customer experience undermines long-term retail growth. While operational efficiency is necessary, reducing headcount and service capabilities without strategic investment damages customer loyalty and brand differentiation. Retailers should instead use technology and AI to support employees and enhance experiences rather than simply cutting costs.
