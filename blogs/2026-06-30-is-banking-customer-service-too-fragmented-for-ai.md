Banks struggle to leverage customer intelligence trapped across disconnected systems. The article argues that interpreting interaction data in real-time, rather than after the fact, lets banks respond faster to customer needs while managing risk and identifying growth opportunities. Talkdesk's approach coordinates action across banking operations at the interaction layer without replacing existing infrastructure.
